Questions

Fair questions.

Anything else, call or email Miranda. A real person answers.

Are the bins actually clean?

Yes. Every bin is cleaned and sanitized after every rental, so nothing from the last move comes along on yours.

How long can I keep them?

Up to 3 weeks. If your closing date moves or you need more time, ask. Packwise can help there too.

Do delivery and pickup cost extra?

No. Delivery and pickup are included in every plan.

How many bins do I need?

10 bins for a studio, dorm or remodeling project. 20 for 1 to 2 bedrooms. 40 for 2 to 3 bedrooms. 60 for 4+ bedrooms, bigger moves and commercial moves. Short a few? Extra bins are one ask away.

Can I get packing paper, bubble wrap or tape?

Yes. Paper, bubble wrap, tape and mattress covers are available as add-ons. Mention what you need when you request your bins.

Where do you deliver?

Packwise serves Greater Pittsburgh. Not sure if your address is covered? Call 412-444-8833 and ask.

Will they hold up in the rain?

They're sturdy, stackable and water-resistant. A soggy driveway won't turn them to mush the way it does cardboard.
